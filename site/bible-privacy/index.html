Bible by OakenCode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bible by OakenCode collects no data.

Works fully offline

The app works fully offline. The Bible texts are stored on your device.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app asks for no account, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

Links

The app contains no links that collect personal information.

Data deletion

There is nothing to delete. No data ever leaves your device, so nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes everything on your device.
