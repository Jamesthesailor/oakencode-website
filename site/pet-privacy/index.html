Pet Health Vault by OakenCode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pet Health Vault by OakenCode collects no data.

Works fully offline

The app works fully offline. Your pet profiles, photos, health records, documents, reminders, and logs are stored on your device.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app asks for no account, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

Your pet's data stays yours

Everything you enter — profiles, breeds, allergies, vaccinations, vet visits, medications, weights, feeding, water, and walk logs, diary notes, photos, and vault documents — stays on your device. We cannot see it, and there is no way for us to recover it. A backup export is a file you create and control; the PDF kit and ZIP export generate files on your device, for you to share or print as you choose.

Links

The app contains no links that collect personal information.

Data deletion

There is nothing to delete. No data ever leaves your device, so nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes everything on your device. A backup export is a file you create and control; delete that file to delete the backup.

Questions about this policy: contact@oakencode.com.
