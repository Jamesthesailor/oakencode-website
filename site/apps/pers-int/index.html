Pers-Int is a personal OSINT tool. OSINT means open-source intelligence: public information, gathered from public places. Pers-Int turns it on yourself, so you can see what the internet already knows about you before anyone else uses it against you. Search your username across 5,000 public sites, check a photo for hidden GPS location data before you share it, and check your email and password against known breaches. Your family dossier stays on your phone, and nowhere else.

What is personal OSINT?

OSINT is open-source intelligence — facts gathered from public websites, public records, and public posts. A digital footprint is the trail those facts leave behind: every account, every photo, every breach with your name on it. Pers-Int gathers that trail for one person — you — and shows it to you first, in plain words, with the most reliable results first.

Try it now — check a photo before you share it

Pick a photo from your device. This page reads the hidden data inside it — GPS location, camera, and date — and shows you what it finds. Your photo never leaves your device. The check runs entirely in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or stored.
