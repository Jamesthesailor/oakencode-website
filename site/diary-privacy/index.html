Private Diary by OakenCode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Private Diary by OakenCode collects no data.

Works fully offline

The app works fully offline. Your diary entries are stored on your device.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app asks for no account, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

Encryption and your PIN

Entries are encrypted on your device with AES-256 and protected by a PIN you choose. We cannot read your entries, and there is no way to recover them if you forget your PIN. There is no back door, because a back door would make the lock a lie.

Links

The app contains no links that collect personal information.

Data deletion

There is nothing to delete. No data ever leaves your device, so nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes everything on your device. A backup export is a file you create and control; delete that file to delete the backup.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@oakencode.com.
