About these texts

Every text here is public domain and free to share: the King James Version (1611); the World English Bible, a modern public-domain translation published by eBible.org; Brenton’s English Septuagint (1844), from eBible.org; and the Hebrew Torah from the Westminster Leningrad Codex, courtesy of Tanach.us. Full provenance is recorded in SOURCES.md. Where the Greek Old Testament numbers chapters differently from the Hebrew — most visibly in the Psalms — the parallel view matches chapters to the same psalm and says so.
